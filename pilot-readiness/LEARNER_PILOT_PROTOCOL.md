# Learner pilot protocol (proposed; no learners involved)

## Design
- One supervised class, one comparison class where feasible, with a qualified teacher and local education partner.
- Pre-register the primary outcome before teaching.
- Baseline assessment, independent post-assessment, delayed retention assessment (at least 4 weeks).
- Include transfer items not identical to practice items; do not use practice questions as the outcome.
- Record attendance, device/connectivity, time on task, abandonment and technical failures.

## Analysis
- Report descriptive pre/post results separately from any causal claim.
- Do not infer causation from a simple pre/post gain.
- Report null and negative outcomes, and the learners who dropped out.
- A qualified educator/researcher selects the assessments and interprets the results.

## Ethics
- No real children’s data during development or automated tests.
- Consent/permission, safeguarding and deletion must be agreed with the school and local organisation.
- No learning-effectiveness claim may be published before this evidence exists.
