or a review. A message becomes a contact only when the owner actually sends it; a

Use one message at a time. Replace `[your name]`, `[your role]` and

## Rules

- **No pupil data.** Do not ask for names, marks, school data or examples from a
  real class. The reviewer's name, role, subject and date are the only personal
  details recorded.
- **No learner testing.** `LEARNER-TESTING-PROTOCOL.md` is a draft and not
  approved. This programme is a teacher reading and using the lesson, not a class
  trial.
- **No invented review.** A reply is not a review. A demo click is not a review.
  Only the signed pack and form are.
- **A "not yet" is a result.** Record it as `not-yet`; do not ask the reviewer to
  soften it.
- **One correction, two places.** If a reviewer identifies a subject error, fix
  the lesson and the pack together, then record the correction and its status.
- **Keep the demo isolated.** The scratch account and store are in
  `EDUCATOR-DEMO.md`. Do not point a reviewer at the live learner store.
