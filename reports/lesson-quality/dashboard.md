## How to read this

- **Words, paragraphs, representations and figures** measure the presence of teaching, not its correctness. A long explanation can be wrong.
- **Why steps** counts worked-example moves whose reason is at least eight words. A step that says only `substitute` is arithmetic wearing a reason's label.
- **Misconceptions** are counted; the lesson gate separately checks that each has a correction and a contrast pair.
- **Reflect** counts explicitly unscored constructed responses. They are not gradable items and are excluded from mastery; the Constructed column still counts them because that is their form.
- **Teacher** and **Learner** are the only columns that require evidence this repository cannot generate. They are counts of real records, and today they are zero.
- No column is a mark, a grade or a prediction. The dashboard exists to make the gap between `machine_checked` and `educator_reviewed` visible, not to close it with a number.

This report is not read by a gate. Re-run it after a lesson change; a stale dashboard is a stale claim.
