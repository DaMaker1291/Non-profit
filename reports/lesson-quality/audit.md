## What this measures

Structural audit of all genome concepts against the authored-lesson contract and the real plan table. It does not measure learning, and it does not create human review or learner evidence.

A concept can be readable and still fail an independent learner. The audit therefore reports the **authored-lesson contract** separately from the plan outline, and never treats a plan record as a lesson.

## How to read the labels

- **`critical`** means a stage-1 concept has no authored lesson. It is a queue position, not a judgment that the existing plan is educationally worthless.
- **`high`** means a later-stage concept has no authored lesson. It is lower in the queue, not a claim that its current page is adequate.
- **`review`** means an authored lesson has every required structural part. It does **not** mean the lesson has been reviewed by a teacher or tested with learners.
- **`machine_checked`** means the contract and deterministic checks pass. It is not a substitute for subject-teacher judgement or evidence of learning.
- The **defect counts** count missing structural parts across 135 concepts. They are not 135 independent quality scores, and a lesson can pass every structural check while containing one serious factual error.

- No learner outcome is claimed. Delayed retention and transfer require supervised testing.
