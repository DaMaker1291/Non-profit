## Findings

1. **13 is a count of CONCEPTS, not items.** 13 concepts are reachable in Foundation and they carry 73 of the 149 items; Higher carries 76. A reviewer is reading a 149-item sample, not a 149-item assessment bank.
2. **Higher is almost entirely multiple choice.** 65 of 76 Higher rows are multiple choice and 11 are numeric entry, while Foundation is 48 numeric and 25 choice. Higher learners are asked to RECOGNISE an answer far more often than to construct one — the opposite of the assessment the roadmap's Stage 3 asks for.
3. **0 of 149 explanations are under 25 prose words.** Every explanation in the pack is at least 25 prose words long, so a reviewer is not handed a bare one-line key.
4. **The pack is a sample, not a bank.** Every tier has at least four items, but most have six; six draws can show that the generator varies, and cannot show that a lesson has enough assessment.
5. **Tier articulation is asymmetric.** `completing-square` and `surds` are Higher-only; `negatives` and `order-ops` are Foundation-only. That is correct curriculum placement, but the pack has no same-concept comparison across tiers for those 4.
